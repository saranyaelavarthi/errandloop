# First independent user trial

Status: prepared; no independent trial results have been collected.

## Set up

Use `python scripts/run_local.py` for a persistent group. Three people can take
turns on the same laptop using separate browser profiles. Each person should
operate their own account. Localhost links do not work on their phones.
The `--rehearsal` mode is useful for a recorded walkthrough, but its prepared
trips and simulated goods are not evidence of real use.

Ask three people you know whether they actually have compatible planned trips
and prepared pickups today. If they do not, record that outcome rather than
inventing demand. Agree on one handover point. Obtain permission before
recording their screens or quoting their feedback.

## Give participants this task

“You are already going somewhere and need an item from another stop. Join this
group, add your trip and request, and see whether everyone can help one another.
Review who will collect your item and what you will collect. Decide whether you
want to accept. Mark a collection only after collecting, and confirm receipt
only after receiving your own item.”

Let participants try without coaching. If they get stuck, note the screen and
what they expected before helping. Do not give them your password.

Keep participant identities and item details private. Use pseudonyms in your
submission. Report small sample size and whether you observed the handovers.
In-app confirmations are participant reports, not independent verification.

## Turn findings into changes

Fix any inability to join, propose, accept or confirm before cosmetic changes.
Repeat the failed step with the participant. Record both the original failure
and the outcome after the fix. If no matching circle exists, ask whether the
app's explanation helped them understand why; do not count it as a delivery.

For the submission, a truthful result might be: “Three people tried the app on
one laptop. Two joined unaided; one needed help finding the group code.” Only
replace these example numbers with observations you actually collected.
